The Most Common Faucet Problems We See

A dripping faucet almost always comes down to a worn cartridge, O-ring, or valve seat, wear items that fail with age regardless of the faucet's quality. Low water pressure at a single faucet often means a clogged aerator, an easy fix, though it can also point to a supply line issue if it's happened suddenly rather than gradually. A faucet that's loose at the base usually just needs the mounting hardware tightened or replaced.

Pottstown's moderately hard water accelerates mineral buildup inside faucet cartridges and aerators specifically, which is part of why a fixture that's fine for years can suddenly start dripping or losing pressure once scale finally clogs the internal passages. In areas with notably harder water, like our Boyertown coverage area, a water softener often cuts down on how often this kind of repair comes up in the first place.

A kitchen faucet with a pull-down sprayer has additional wear points, the hose and the diverter valve inside the spout, that a standard two-handle bathroom faucet doesn't have, which is why a kitchen faucet repair sometimes involves a different diagnosis than the same symptom on a bathroom sink.

Sink Leaks: Finding The Actual Source

A puddle under the sink can come from the supply lines, the drain trap, the garbage disposal gasket, or the faucet's own base gasket, and each has a different fix. We trace the leak to its actual source with the water running under normal use conditions rather than guessing from where the water pooled, since water travels along cabinet surfaces before it puddles.

A slow drip that only shows up after the sink has been used a few times, rather than immediately, often means the leak is in the drain trap connection rather than the supply lines, a distinction that changes where we start looking first.

Repair Or Replace

Most faucet issues are a straightforward parts repair. A faucet with a cracked body, a finish that's failing, or one simply old enough that replacement parts are hard to source is when we discuss a full fixture replacement instead. Installation of a customer-selected faucet is available whether you've already bought one or want help choosing.

A discontinued faucet line from a builder-grade installation two decades ago is a common reason replacement parts simply aren't available anymore, at which point matching the exact original fixture stops being realistic and a full swap becomes the practical answer.

We keep a range of common cartridge and valve types on the truck specifically because same-day repair depends on having the right part on hand rather than making a second trip once the exact model is identified.

That inventory approach is part of why most faucet repairs finish in a single visit, without the delay and inconvenience of scheduling a separate follow-up appointment once a part arrives.

Before We Arrive

Locate the shutoff valves under the sink

Most sinks have individual hot and cold shutoffs; know where they are before an active leak.

Clear out the cabinet below

A clear workspace speeds up diagnosis and repair.

Note when the problem started

A sudden leak versus a gradual drip point to different causes.

Don't use excessive force on a stuck handle

Forcing a seized faucet handle can crack the fixture body, turning a repair into a replacement.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does my faucet drip even after I replaced the washer?

Modern faucets often use a cartridge instead of a simple washer; replacing the wrong part won't fix a cartridge-based drip.

Is a dripping faucet actually worth fixing?

Yes. A faucet dripping once per second wastes over 3,000 gallons a year, a real, avoidable cost.

Do you install faucets I've already purchased?

Yes, we can install a customer-supplied faucet or help you select one during the visit.

Why is my sink draining slowly even though it's not fully clogged?

Partial buildup in the trap or a slow-forming clog further down the line often causes this before a full blockage develops.

Can you match the finish of my existing fixtures?

Yes, we source replacement faucets and fixtures in matching finishes whenever a specific match is important to you.
