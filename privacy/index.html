Privacy Policy

Effective 2026. This policy explains how Pottstown Express Plumbing handles information collected when you contact us.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when you call us, such as your name, phone number, service address, and a description of the plumbing issue. We do not use web forms on this site, so no information is collected through form submissions. Standard web server logs may record basic technical information such as IP address and browser type for security and site performance purposes.

How We Use Information

Information collected during a phone call is used solely to schedule, dispatch, and invoice plumbing services. We do not sell customer information to third parties.

Call Recording & Tracking

Calls to our published phone numbers may be recorded or tracked for quality assurance and service attribution purposes. By calling, you consent to this practice, consistent with applicable Pennsylvania law.

Third-Party Services

This site may use third-party analytics and mapping services (such as Google Maps embeds). These services may collect information according to their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review separately.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy can be directed to contact@expressplumberpottstown.com or by calling (610) 672-7601.
