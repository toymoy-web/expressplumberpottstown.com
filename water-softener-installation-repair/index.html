Pottstown's water runs moderately hard, and it gets genuinely harder the further out you go, with private wells in areas like Boyertown regularly testing in the very-hard range.

Why Hardness Varies So Much Across Our Service Area

Pottstown's municipal water carries moderate hardness, enough to cause water heater sediment buildup and mineral scale over years, but not severe. Private wells in outlying areas, including parts of our Boyertown service area drawing from local bedrock aquifers, commonly test 10 to 20 grains per gallon, solidly in the "very hard" category. A softener sized for Pottstown's municipal water would genuinely underperform on a Boyertown-area well, which is why we test before recommending a system rather than install the same unit everywhere.

Hardness can even vary within the same street, particularly on private wells where two neighboring properties draw from slightly different points in the same bedrock aquifer. That variability is exactly why we test each specific property rather than rely on a neighbor's results or a general area estimate.

Signs You Need A Softener, Or A Repair On One You Have

Chalky white buildup on faucets and showerheads, soap that won't lather properly, dishes with spots even after the dishwasher runs, and laundry that feels stiff are the classic hard-water signs. If you already have a softener and it's stopped resolving these symptoms, a depleted or malfunctioning resin bed, a stuck valve, or simply running out of salt are the usual culprits.

Reduced water pressure that develops gradually over months, not suddenly, can also point to mineral scale building up inside pipes and fixtures faster than expected, a sign that existing softening isn't keeping pace with your actual water hardness anymore.

Sizing A System To Your Actual Water

A softener sized by grains-per-day capacity needs your actual water hardness and household water usage, not a generic household-size estimate. We test your water directly rather than assume based on whether you're on municipal or well water, since even within Boyertown, hardness varies meaningfully between borough water and surrounding private wells. Softener installation follows the International Plumbing Code (IPC) requirements for point-of-entry treatment systems, including proper bypass valve installation for maintenance access.

An undersized system regenerates too frequently, wasting salt and water; an oversized one costs more upfront than necessary. Getting the sizing right the first time, based on real tested numbers, avoids both of those ongoing inefficiencies. A household also dealing with taste or odor complaints beyond hardness may want to look at water filtration alongside a softener, since the two solve genuinely different problems.

We also check your home's water pressure and flow rate during installation, since a softener adds a small amount of pressure drop across the system, and confirming your baseline pressure can handle that before installation avoids an unpleasant surprise afterward.

What To Check Before You Call

Note whether you're on well or municipal water

This changes both expected hardness and the recommended system type.

Look for visible scale buildup

Chalky residue on faucets and showerheads is a quick visual indicator worth mentioning.

Check if you already have a softener

A sudden change can point to a specific new issue worth testing for.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if I need a softener?

Chalky buildup on fixtures, soap that won't lather, and spotty dishes are the common signs. A quick water test confirms it and tells us how severe it is.

Is Boyertown's water really that much harder than Pottstown's?

Often yes, for homes on private wells in the surrounding townships, which commonly test 10 to 20 grains per gallon versus Pottstown's more moderate municipal hardness.

How often does a softener need maintenance?

Salt needs regular refilling, and the resin bed typically needs attention every several years depending on usage and water hardness.

Can hard water actually damage my plumbing?

Yes, over time mineral scale narrows pipe diameter and shortens the lifespan of water heaters and fixtures, making softening a genuine long-term investment, not just a comfort upgrade.

Do you service softener brands you didn't originally install?

Yes, we repair and maintain most major softener brands regardless of who installed them originally.
